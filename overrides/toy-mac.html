THE ORIGINAL MACINTOSH TOY

This is the preserved 1998 sequencer, inside an emulated Power Macintosh running Mac OS 8.1.

Click to start. The classic Mac will load from Infinite Mac.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Once Mac OS 8.1 boots, open the desktop disk labelled DAFT PUNK TOY.
2. Double-click phoenix in that disk to start the original program.
3. Click or tap inside the emulator if sound is muted. This vintage program may still have compatibility limitations.
On a phone, landscape orientation is recommended.
